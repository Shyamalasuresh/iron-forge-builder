Our Story

Built in the
Fire of Discipline.

Founded in 2008 by powerlifters who were tired of polished commercial gyms, Iron Forge was built on one principle: show up and do the work.

We don't sell ambiance. We sell results. Our 25,000 sq ft warehouse facility houses the heaviest plates, the loudest barbells, and the most relentless community in the city.
